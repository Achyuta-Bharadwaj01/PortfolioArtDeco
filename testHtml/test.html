The Study

Before I design a room, I try to understand a person.

I'm Shaineela — an interior architect, stylist, and creative strategist, but before any of those titles, I'm someone who has always noticed how spaces make people feel, long before I had language for it.

Nine years in, across residential design, hospitality, styling, and business strategy, I've learned the work I care about most isn't about trends or square footage. It's about identity — helping a person, brand, or home say something true about itself.

What I Stand For
I.

Space is identity

A room should tell the truth about the person living in it — not the trend they were sold.

II.

Nature is not decoration

Biophilic design isn't a plant in the corner. It's a way of letting a space breathe.

III.

More can be honest

I don't believe in minimalism for its own sake. Layered and romantic can still be true.
